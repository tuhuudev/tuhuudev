Under the hood

How this site is built

  • Custom GLSL shadersSimplex-noise vertex displacement and fresnel shading on the hero core
  • Scroll-synced WebGLOne fixed canvas; each section owns a 3D scene placed at its exact page position
  • Adaptive qualityFrame-time monitor lowers pixel ratio and turns off bloom on slower devices
  • Code splittingPage text renders first; Three.js loads in its own chunk afterwards
  • Smooth scrollingLenis inertial scroll driven from the same requestAnimationFrame as rendering
  • Accessible fallbacksHonors prefers-reduced-motion and works without WebGL
